# Fight & High-Action Scene Protocol

**MANDATORY for any fight, combat, chase, rapid action, or intense physical confrontation.** Never summarize fight scenes in one or two generic sentences. Always break into detailed multi-shot sequences.

## Universal Fighting Storyboard Pattern (万能打斗分镜)

Every fight scene must follow this structure:

1. **Wide/Establishing (全景)**: Establish positions, environment, number of fighters, spatial relationship
2. **Medium (中景)**: Capture complete actions and full body movement
3. **Close-up (近景)**: Fast cuts for reactions, micro-expressions, precise strikes/dodges
4. **Extreme Close-up/Insert (特写)**: Impacts, blood/snow splatter, weapon collisions, eye details, pain/fear expressions
5. **Wide/Result (全景)**: Show outcome, separation, lingering tension

## Fast-Cut Sequences

For rapid multi-shot quick cuts (typically 0.3-0.6s each):
- Explicitly list multiple short shots with precise sub-timing allocation
- **Shot-reverse-shot (正反打) MUST be broken into individual alternating shots**: Never group as one item. Clearly separate A-side (e.g., Lin Chong deadpan eyes + precise strike) and B-side (e.g., villain fear expression + dodge), each with own timing, specific action, micro-expression, description
- Specific actions: eye expression (deadpan, fear), precise strikes, dodges, collisions, splatter physics
- Sound design: dense Foley impacts, panting, wind/howling, breathing
- Cinematic lighting using professional terms (侧逆光 + 雪地反光, 顶光压抑 + 侧光戏剧性)
- Integrate full realism: physics of impacts, fabric inertia, material interactions (snow flying, blood on snow, wet surfaces)

## Handheld Photography for Realism (手持摄影真实感规则)

### Rules
- Use **motivated handheld only**: Apply when enhancing physical weight, fatigue, chaos, or subjective emotional experience. Never random or excessive shake.
- **Specify intensity and motivation** in every action shot description:
  - Light/natural breathing shake: Conveys exhaustion, tension, or character's physical state
  - Moderate handheld + fast cuts: Heightens immediacy and violence in close combat or impacts
  - Avoid over-shaky "found footage" style unless intentionally documentary-like

### Combine with Lighting & Physics
- Handheld + 侧逆光 / 侧光: Creates dynamic shifting shadows and rim light during movement
- Handheld + 雪地/雨天/血迹: Amplifies realistic particle movement, splatter physics, and material interaction

### Prompt Language
Include: "motivated handheld with subtle natural breathing shake", "dynamic organic camera movement", "handheld texture enhancing physical realism and fatigue"

### Balance
Use handheld primarily in near/extreme close-up reaction and impact moments. Use more controlled/Steadicam shots for establishing geography and full-body action.

## Action Cinematography Aesthetics (电影动作运镜美学规则)

### Rhythm & Pacing
- Master dialectic of fast cuts vs. long takes: rapid multi-shot quick cuts (0.3-0.6s) for adrenaline; motivated long takes for weight, fatigue, spatial clarity
- Create deliberate rhythm contrast: dense fast-cut sequences followed by brief slower/held shot for emotional breathing room

### Spatial Choreography
- Always prioritize clear geography. Follow Universal Fighting Storyboard Pattern strictly
- Use camera movement to actively reveal or maintain spatial relationships

### Lighting & Movement Synergy
- Camera movement and lighting designed together. Use professional lighting positions in sync with camera motion

### Emotional & Physical Motivation
- Every camera movement motivated by emotion, body state, or story beat
- Eye-driven: show deadpan eyes first, then follow precise strike
- Weight-driven: slight handheld conveys fatigue and inertia of staff/blade

## Classic 9-Shot Fight Progression Template (经典9分镜打斗结构)

1. **SCENE 01 - Setup/Approach**: Establish characters, location, initial tension (one side unaware or distant)
2. **SCENE 02 - Initial Attack**: Antagonist launches first aggressive move; protagonist reacts/defends
3. **SCENE 03 - Close Struggle**: Intense physical contact, hand-to-hand or weapon clash in tight framing
4. **SCENE 04 - Counter / Dynamic Strike**: Protagonist lands significant counter-attack, shifting momentum
5. **SCENE 05 - Grappling / Environmental Combat**: Close-quarters struggle using walls, ground, confined space
6. **SCENE 06 - Improvised Weapon**: Protagonist acquires/uses environmental object as weapon, escalating fight
7. **SCENE 07 - Brutal Climax**: Most violent/decisive moment (heavy strike, choke, blood impact in close-up)
8. **SCENE 08 - Dominance**: Protagonist clearly dominates or finishes opponent
9. **SCENE 09 - Aftermath/Resolution**: Protagonist walks away/exits; antagonist defeated. Visual and emotional closure.

For meaningful fights, expand to 7-9+ detailed shots following this emotional and visual arc.

## Prompt Integration for Action Shots

In every AI Image Prompt for action shots, include:
- "rapid multi-shot quick cuts, shot-reverse-shot fast editing"
- "dense Foley sound design"
- "precise physical collision"
- Material interaction relevant to environment ("snow and blood splatter physics", "rain and wet surface physics")

## Self-Check for Fights

Confirm every described action, expression, impact, and sound cue has its own dedicated shot(s). No omissions. When 正反打 or fast editing is used, verify each side of the reverse shot explicitly broken out as separate shots with individual timing and content.
