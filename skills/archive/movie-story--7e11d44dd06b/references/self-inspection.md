# Post-Generation Quality Self-Inspection & Upgrade Protocol

生成后质感自检与升级 — 强制执行

## Self-Inspection Checklist (逐项检查)

After completing storyboard and prompt generation, perform the following quality self-inspection:

1. **Lighting**: Does lighting have cinematic-level layering and volumetric quality? (Clear key, rim, ambient light interaction?)
2. **Character Texture**: Is character texture premium? (Skin pores, eye focus and catchlight consistency, fabric natural folds and weight, hair strand detail?)
3. **Environment & Physics**: Is environmental and physical feedback realistic? (Particle movement, material interaction, wet/dry texture, reflection logic?)
4. **AI Artifacts**: Are there obvious AI fake qualities? (Plastic skin, vacant eyes, stiff poses, floating focus, inconsistent highlights?)
5. **Emotion & Performance**: Is emotion and performance natural with layers? (Micro-expressions, subconscious micro-movements, emotional landing clear?)
6. **Production Standard**: Does the overall quality meet "directly usable for film-grade image/video generation" standard?

## Upgrade Rules

If self-inspection finds average texture or obvious problems, immediately self-upgrade prompts:
- Strengthen lighting description (add specific light positions + volumetric light + film references)
- Enhance eye and skin realism (use Ocular Realism module from photorealism-layer.md)
- Increase material and physical detail (fabric weight, particle interaction, subsurface scattering)
- Elevate performance structure (more specific "character + micro-movement + subconscious reaction + emotional landing")

After upgrading, re-inspect until texture reaches professional standard.

## Final Output Requirement

All prompts must pass this self-inspection and upgrade process. Output may include brief self-inspection note (e.g., "已自检并强化眼睛汇聚与光影层次").
