# Character & Scene Consistency Bible

## Character Consistency Bible (MANDATORY for multi-shot projects)

When processing the first highly detailed shot, immediately extract and permanently lock a rich **Character Design Bible**.

### Elements to Lock
- Multiple angles: Front, 3/4, Side, Back, Hair/Accessory details
- Close-up detail grids: Embroidery motifs, collar, jewelry, fabric texture, fur
- Full-body views from different angles
- Material & texture specifics (velvet, gold/silver embroidery, jade, pearls, fur)
- Hair style and accessory breakdown
- Color palette and overall mood

Every future prompt must reference these locked elements for strict visual consistency.

### Example (Hero Character)
"Johnny: mid-30s East Asian male, sharp jawline, intense focused eyes with natural micro-expressions, light stubble, visible skin pores and subtle texture under cold light, natural slight redness on cheeks. Wearing heavy dark hooded monastic robe with thick matte woven fabric, deep natural folds and realistic weight, slight damp darkening at hem. Red tactical goggles with micro-scratches and soft reflections worn on face. Hands show real skin texture, knuckles, and subtle calluses. Body type: athletic and grounded. Expression style: restrained, ritualistic, absolute resolve. No changes to face structure, robe details, goggle appearance, or skin texture across any shots."

### Example (Powerful Corporate Villain)
"Powerful corporate villain male in his mid-40s, refined and luxurious appearance with sharp, aristocratic features. Wearing an expensive custom-tailored black suit with subtle sheen and fine fabric texture, slicked-back glossy black hair with precise styling, cold arrogant expression with a subtle sinister half-smile at the corner of his mouth. Wearing a luxurious high-end watch with metallic reflections. Seated in a high-end executive leather chair. Located in a sleek glass-walled skyscraper office with panoramic night city views. Overall atmosphere: cold, oppressive yet restrained business empire aesthetic with strong presence."

## Standardized Character Description Template

Use this structured format for all major characters:

"[Archetype + Age + Gender], [key facial features], [expression/micro-expression details]. Wearing [clothing with fabric texture, fit, and details]. [Hair style and texture]. [Key accessories with material/reflection details]. [Posture/action if applicable]. Located in [environment/setting]. Overall atmosphere: [mood + presence]. Cinematic lighting with [specific light interaction]. Ultra-realistic movie still quality, [skin texture, pores, imperfections], precise material rendering on [clothing/accessories]."

## Scene Consistency Bible

When processing the first highly detailed shot of a location, immediately extract and permanently lock a rich **Scene Bible**. Every future prompt for the same location MUST match exactly in: architecture, lighting direction & color temperature, props placement, atmosphere, overall mood.

### 16-Grid Multi-Angle Scene Reference System
For important or multi-shot locations, establish a 16-grid multi-angle reference system (360° different camera positions):
- 4 main positions (four corners / key locations) × 4 distance/height combinations (wide, medium, close-up, detail angle)
- Systematic coverage: room corners + center + different heights (low angle, eye level, high angle)

### Example (Monastery Nave)
"Interior of vast ancient monastery nave at night: wet stone floor with realistic puddles and blue reflections, ancient stone columns and arches with mineral streaks and wear, high vaulted ceiling fading into cold blue mist. Thick cold blue-gray fog (7000K-8000K) rising from floor, swirling around legs and waist, volumetric god rays, floating dust and condensation particles. Cold directional moonlight through arched stained-glass windows, strong but softened by fog. Low-key cinematic blue-gray color grade, high contrast, deep blacks, subtle film grain, soft lens flare on window highlights. No modern elements, no text, extreme photorealistic monastery atmosphere with oppressive ritual tension."

## Prop Design & Consistency Template

When a prop is central to the story or appears across multiple shots:

**Prop Name**: [name]
**Hero Render**: Main beauty shot — overall form, material finish, light interaction, dominant aesthetic
**Key Details & Ornaments**: Engravings, inlays, charms, mechanical features, textures
**Dimensions**: Precise measurements in cm
**Materials & Finish**: Base material + surface treatment
**Color Palette**: 4-6 colors with descriptive names
**Structure Breakdown**: How it disassembles or key functional parts
**Design References**: Historical, cultural, real-world inspiration
**Usage & Interaction**: How prop behaves with characters, lighting, action, environment

## Consistency Enforcement in Every AI Image Prompt

After main description, ALWAYS append:
"Strict character consistency: exactly matching the locked Character Bible — no drift in face, skin texture, clothing fabric/weave/folds/weight, accessories, or body language. Strict scene consistency: exactly matching the locked Scene Bible — identical architecture, stone texture, lighting direction and color temperature, fog density and movement, floor reflections, and overall atmosphere."
