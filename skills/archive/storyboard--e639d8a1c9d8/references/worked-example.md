# Worked Example — Coffee Shop Scene

## Script

```
INT. COFFEE SHOP — NIGHT

MAYA sits alone at a corner table, phone face-down, hands flat.

JAKE enters, scanning the room. He spots her. He crosses to the table.

JAKE
I thought you weren't coming.

MAYA
(not looking up)
I almost didn't.

She finally looks up. They hold eye contact.
```

## Step 3 — Analysis

```
SCENE ANALYSIS: INT. COFFEE SHOP — NIGHT
  Type: ROMANCE · Law: SCIAMMA (gaze structure governs scene)
  Surface: Jake arrives, two lines spoken · Subtext: a test of whether she'll stay
  Maya WANTS: not to need this / NEEDS: to let him in
  Jake WANTS: reconnect / NEEDS: earn trust
  Power: Maya → shifts at look-up → EQUAL
  Arc: guarded → she looks up → mutual exposure
  Camera: WS → MS → CU at look-up → ECU held
  Contract: 2/4/1/1 · Shots: 7 · ≥65% = ≥5 reaction

ROMANCE: Gaze: watcher=JAKE → reversal at look-up → MUTUAL
  Distance: across table throughout (gaze IS the distance)
  Involuntary: Maya fingers press flat · Jake jaw releases
  Sound: NO score · diegetic=café hum · amplified=her exhale at look-up
  Empty frame: NO (scene ends on contact)
```
