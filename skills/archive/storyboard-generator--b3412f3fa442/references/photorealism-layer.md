# Photorealism & Anti-AI-Plastic Layer

**MANDATORY for all human character shots.** Integrate these techniques into every AI Image Prompt to eliminate plastic skin, vacant eyes, stiff poses, flat lighting, and "AI-generated" artifacts.

## Expression & Performance Structure (表情与表演结构)

Use the structure: **"Character + small ongoing action + subconscious reaction + emotional landing point"** to guide organic performance and eliminate stiff, vacant AI poses.

Format: `[Character] engages in [small ongoing action], [subconscious micro-reaction visible in eyes/body], [emotional arc landing from state A to state B].`

For video prompts, especially emphasize natural blinking and breathing.

## Action & Inertia (动作与惯性)

Movements have force and weight. Clothing, hair, and fabric edges trail with natural inertia. Add subconscious micro-actions: fingertip tremors, slight shoulder shrugs, breathing-induced torso rise/fall. Avoid rigid, frozen poses.

## Lighting & Contrast (光源与明暗)

The frame must have clear light/shadow variation. Use dramatic lighting: top light + hair light + side light + natural light particles. Never use flat, even illumination. Preserve authentic light-shadow layers and atmosphere (dust grain, light particles). Prefer professional film lighting terminology.

## Skin & Facial Realism (皮肤与面部真实感)

- Visible pores and fine lines
- Natural rosy undertones, uneven skin tone (subtle color variation)
- Keep subtle moles/freckles, authentic skin texture, clear pores
- Translucent complexion, not unnaturally pale
- Facial features naturally organic with slight asymmetry
- Relaxed, non-stiff expression
- **ABSOLUTELY NO plastic/ceramic skin or AI modeling artifacts**

## Ocular Realism (眼睛真实感 — 最高优先级)

AI eyes most often fail on **ocular focus logic**, not resolution. Real eyes project consciousness; AI eyes are often just textures.

### Binocular Vergence (双眼汇聚一致性)
Both eyes must lock onto the same focal target. Near-distance = noticeable convergence. Far-distance = near parallel. **Forbidden**: left eye looking left, right eye looking right, or floating focus.

### Catchlight Anchor (高光锚点一致)
Both eyes' highlight positions must strictly correspond to the main light direction. Logical unity.

### Corneal Refraction & Volume (角膜折射与体积感)
Cornea has convex thickness, pupil has depth, highlights float on surface layer, iris has spatial depth (NOT a flat texture map).

### Microsaccades & Pupil Dynamics (微扫视与动态)
Include subtle eye tremors, pupil response to emotion/light, breathing rhythm.

### Eyelid-Eye Movement Coordination (眼睑与眼球联动)
When eyes move, eyelids have natural compression and tension changes.

### Consciousness-Oriented Description (意识导向描述 — 最重要)
Prompts MUST include "conscious gaze / focused attention / emotionally anchored eye focus / locked on specific target", NOT just "detailed eyes."

### Recommended Ocular Prompt Block
```
perfect binocular convergence, both eyes locked on same focal target, realistic corneal refraction, physically accurate catchlights, subtle tear film reflection, natural pupil depth, cinematic ocular tension, microsaccadic eye movement, emotionally focused gaze, dynamic eyelid compression, living human eyes, no dead-eye effect, conscious attention
```

## Tears & Emotional Fluid Realism (眼泪与情绪液体真实感)

Apply ONLY when shots involve crying, emotional breakdown, or emotional climax close-ups.

Real human tears: overflow slowly from inner corner of eye, translucent saline texture, subtle environmental light reflections on surface, flow naturally down cheek following gravity in irregular curve, pool at cheekbone then drip off, wet the skin creating moist reflective patches, slightly smudge mascara, leave faint tear tracks, small tear droplets cling to eyelashes. Facial muscles naturally tense, lips tremble subtly, overall emotion restrained. **Tears must show authentic saline texture, NOT white solid or unnatural flow.**

## Depth of Field & Composition (景深与构图)

Foreground: blurred obstruction elements (leaves, window frames, character shoulder). Midground: character sharp and crisp. Background: natural blur (wide aperture shallow DOF effect). Use 85mm prime lens simulation, straight-out-of-camera quality.

## Overall Texture (整体质感)

Subtle film grain, 8K resolution, lively natural demeanor, casual/lifelike costuming, authentic real-location background (NOT pure AI composite feel). Reference: "hyperrealistic portrait photography, straight-out-of-camera" style.

## Standard Prompt Structure Order

```
[Cinematic shot description], [performance structure with micro-expression + subconscious reaction + emotional landing], [powerful natural action + clothing inertia], [dramatic lighting with top/hair/side light + particles + dust], [photorealistic skin: visible pores, fine lines, natural rosy uneven tone, subtle moles, no plastic skin], [85mm lens, shallow DOF, foreground blur obstruction, sharp midground subject, blurred background], [film grain, 8K, no AI artifacts, lively natural demeanor, straight-out-of-camera] --ar 2.39:1 --stylize 250 --v 6
```

## Hero/Key Shot Prompt Standard (英雄镜头首帧提示词标准)

For key shots (Hero Shot, emotional/action climax), prompts must reach extreme cinematic technical detail:
- Exact camera/lens specs (model, focal length, aperture, shutter, ISO, camera height, tilt angle)
- Detailed composition and framing logic
- Complete light breakdown (key + rim + fill + color temp + practicals)
- Atmosphere, texture and particle details
- Restrained, ritualistic performance
- Final cinematic effect description

## Video/Multi-Shot Emotional Sequence Standard

For video or multi-shot emotional sequences (especially crying, heartbeat, emotional breakdown performances):

### Global Control
Define overall motion style, camera texture (shaky handheld with organic breathing movement), performance weight, real physics weight. **Mandatory character consistency + real tear physics reference.**

### Shot-by-Shot Breakdown
Each shot independently detailed:
- Focal length + camera movement (slow dolly-in push, organic breathing, micro-shake)
- Performance structure (character + ongoing small action + subconscious reaction + emotional landing)
- Specific micro-expression and muscle dynamic weight
- Real tear physics (viscous flow, gravitational roll, refraction on wet skin)
- Lighting and skin texture details

### Recommended Core Weight Controls
- (highly refined real tear physics:1.55)
- (lighting refraction on wet skin:1.55)
- (specific eye muscle dynamics:1.55)
- (detailed facial muscle micro-expressions:1.45)
- (subtle performance:1.35)
- (platysma neck tension:1.5)
