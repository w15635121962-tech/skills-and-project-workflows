# Cinematic Romance, Ambiguity & Intimacy Scene Protocol

电影级暧昧、恋爱与亲密戏设计协议

## Core Principle

The essence of advanced romance is not "sweetness" or displaying bodies, but shooting **changes in psychological distance**, **desire and restraint**, **breathing rhythm**, and **emotional accumulation**. The camera must shoot "the relationship inching closer" and the tension of "almost but not quite."

## Core Formula

Ambiguity = approaching + restraint. Advanced ambiguity always has the unfinished feeling of "almost touching."

## Sightline & Stolen Glances (视线与偷看)

Advanced romance rarely uses direct eye contact. More often: stolen glances, peripheral vision, mirror reflections, turning back while brushing past. Eye contact duration = emotional density. Slowly looking away after being discovered creates intense atmospheric shift.

## Breathing Lens in Romance (呼吸镜头在爱情戏中的应用)

The lens must behave like someone secretly observing:
- Emotion approaching: extremely slow push-in
- Being discovered: stop
- Losing control: breathing becomes heavier

This makes the audience feel "the air is getting hotter."

## Spatial Distance Design (空间距离设计)

- **Safe distance** (table, door frame, corridor): relationship still rational
- **Dangerous distance** (hands could touch, breath perceptible): relationship beginning to cross boundaries
- Strongest tension from "almost touching": hand hovering, breath approaching, shoulders lightly brush, clothing edges graze past

## Body Language Leaks (身体语言泄漏)

When truly falling for someone, the body becomes honest first:
- Breathing changes, fingers tighten, unconscious hair adjustment, slight step back, gaze pause
- Stepping back is sometimes fear of losing control
- Stopping is the most dangerous state

## Light & Atmosphere (光影与氛围)

- Soft light reduces realism, adds dream quality
- Preserve shadows (half face in darkness, side light cutting) to create secrecy and danger
- Mixed warm/cool light is most classic

## Focal Length & Intimacy (焦距与亲密关系)

- **Wide angle**: First meeting, estrangement
- **Mid focal**: Everyday tenderness
- **Mid-telephoto**: Ambiguity, eye contact, desire pressure. Background blur means "the world begins to only contain each other"

## Wong Kar-wai Style Romance Storyboarding

- Heavy spatial obstruction (narrow corridors, door frames, mirrors)
- The stronger the emotion, the more restrained the camera
- Shoot brushing past, clothing edges, slow-motion turns
- Love changes one's perception of time → use slow motion and repetition

## Environmental Sound Amplification (环境声音放大)

When truly falling for someone, one hears details: breathing sounds, fabric friction, wind, electrical hum. These sounds amplify private space and lonely intimacy.

## Intimacy Scene Design Principles (亲密戏设计原则 — 最高级克制)

- **Never start from "action."** First establish emotion, space, breathing, silence
- Focus on shooting: hands, back of neck, shoulders, breathing, hair, gaze, skin light
- Breathing and pauses are most important (approach → pause → hesitate)
- Lighting: soft side light, shadows wrapping
- Space: quiet, private

## Romance Rhythm Structure

初识 (First Meeting) → 暧昧 Ambiguity (mid-focal slow push + breathing enhanced) → 心动 Heart Flutter (telephoto + world goes out of focus) → 失控 Loss of Control → 平静余韵 Calm Aftermath
