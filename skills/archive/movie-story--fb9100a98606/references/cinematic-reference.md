# Cinematic Reference Guide — Focal Length, Camera, Lighting & Style

## Focal Length Emotional Spectrum

### Ultra-Wide (14-18mm)
**Emotional signature:** Spatial distortion, unease, environmental dominance
**Motion cadence:** Fast, sweeping, disorienting
**Camera behavior:** Wide searching arcs, tilted horizons, dramatic perspective shifts
**Best for:** Chaos/panic, claustrophobia, epic scale contrast, vomit-cam

### Wide (24-28mm)
**Emotional signature:** Environmental context, naturalism, spatial awareness
**Motion cadence:** Flowing, purposeful, following
**Camera behavior:** Gentle tracking, slight drift, responsive reframe
**Best for:** Establishing movement, urban walking, environmental storytelling

### Standard (35mm)
**Emotional signature:** Human-eye naturalism, neutral storytelling
**Motion cadence:** Conversational, organic, subtle
**Camera behavior:** Gentle sway, slight reframe, breathing rhythm
**Best for:** Walk-and-talk, natural interaction, fly-on-the-wall documentary

### Normal (50mm)
**Emotional signature:** Objective truth, documentary reality, journalistic
**Motion cadence:** Measured, intentional, deliberate
**Camera behavior:** Subtle drift, slight push-in on key moments, reactive framing
**Best for:** Interviews, confessions, reality-based storytelling, neutral grounding

### Portrait (85mm)
**Emotional signature:** Intimacy, vulnerability, emotional access
**Motion cadence:** Slow, breath-like, almost tender
**Camera behavior:** Minimal movement, micro-shake, slight parallax on eye-line
**Best for:** Emotional close-ups, key prop emphasis, character depth moments

### Short Telephoto (100-135mm)
**Emotional signature:** Compression, voyeurism, isolation, surveillance
**Motion cadence:** Slow drift, detached observation, tracking
**Camera behavior:** Slow pan, compressed tracking, distant follow
**Best for:** Stalking/surveillance, emotional distance, urban isolation

### Super Telephoto (200mm+)
**Emotional signature:** Extreme compression, dreamlike detachment, unreachable
**Motion cadence:** Glacial, hypnotic, floating
**Camera behavior:** Almost imperceptible drift, heat-haze quality
**Best for:** Dissociation, dream sequences, extreme urban compression

## Camera Movement Vocabulary

### Discovery (camera "finds" the subject)
- `camera finds the subject with slight searching movement`
- `camera pans slowly to discover the subject`
- `camera drifts into frame, settling on the subject`

### Following (camera tracks the subject)
- `camera follows laterally with reactive reframe`
- `camera tracks from behind with organic sway`
- `camera keeps pace while adjusting framing mid-shot`
- `camera weaves through the scene alongside the subject`

### Intimate (camera breathes with subject)
- `intimate handheld proximity with subtle shake`
- `camera drifts almost imperceptibly, matching breath rhythm`
- `micro-adjustments in framing as subject shifts`
- `slight parallax shift creating depth between subject and background`

### Reactive (camera responds to subject)
- `camera pulls back reactively as subject moves forward`
- `camera whips to follow sudden subject movement`
- `camera adjusts framing mid-shot to compensate for subject shift`
- `camera tilts down following the subject's gaze`

### Structural (camera defines the space)
- `camera holds wide while subjects move through the frame`
- `camera slowly pushes in, tightening the frame`
- `camera tilts from ground to horizon, revealing the environment`

### Seedance-Specific Movements
- `camera slowly pushes in` / `dolly in toward subject` — 推镜头 (increasing intimacy)
- `camera pulls back revealing environment` / `dolly out` — 拉镜头 (context reveal)
- `camera pans slowly to the right` — 摇镜头 (scanning, surveying)
- `camera tracks laterally alongside` / `side dolly` — 移镜头 (parallel journey)
- `camera follows closely behind` / `tracking shot` — 跟镜头 (immersion, pursuit)
- `camera slowly rises` / `crane shot ascending` — 升镜头 (grandeur)
- `camera orbits around the subject` — 环绕镜头 (360° emphasis)
- `handheld camera following the action` / `shaky cam` — 手持跟拍 (rawness, urgency)
- `smooth steadicam movement` — 斯坦尼康 (dreamlike elegance)

## Scene Type Quick Templates

### Chase / Pursuit
- Focal progression: 24mm → 50mm → 85mm → 35mm OTS → 135mm → 18mm high-angle
- Rhythm: Cut-heavy, energy building, 2-3 sec average shot

### Tension / Suspicion
- Focal progression: 35mm → 50mm → 135mm → 85mm → 200mm → 24mm reveal
- Rhythm: Long takes, 4-6 sec, hold on faces, delayed cuts

### Emotional Breakdown
- Focal progression: 50mm → 35mm → 85mm → 85mm tighter → 135mm → 24mm environmental
- Rhythm: Lingering, 3-5 sec, cutting on breath

### Action / Combat
- Focal progression: 18mm → 35mm → 50mm → 85mm detail → 24mm wide → 135mm impact
- Rhythm: Rapid cuts, 1-2 sec, match-on-action

### Romance / Connection
- Focal progression: 35mm → 50mm → 85mm → 35mm reverse → 85mm → 24mm pull-back
- Rhythm: Lingering on faces, 3-4 sec, eye-line shifts

### Surveillance / Stalking
- Focal progression: 135mm → 200mm → 85mm close → 50mm discovered → 24mm flee → 135mm aftermath
- Rhythm: Long holds, 5-8 sec, voyeuristic distance

### Walking / Journey
- Focal progression: 24mm → 35mm → 50mm → 35mm OTS → 85mm detail → 18mm arrival
- Rhythm: Flowing, 3-4 sec, movement-matched cuts

## Lighting & Atmosphere Modifiers

### Lighting Direction
- `even front lighting` — 正面光 (flat, beauty)
- `dramatic side lighting` / `Rembrandt lighting` — 侧面光 (chiaroscuro)
- `backlit silhouette` / `rim lighting` — 逆光 (ethereal, silhouette)
- `harsh overhead lighting` — 顶光 (interrogation, noon)

### Lighting Quality
- `hard light with sharp shadows` — 硬光 (noir, high contrast)
- `soft diffused lighting` — 柔光 (romantic, dreamlike)
- `volumetric god rays` / `light shafts cutting through fog` — 体积光 (atmospheric, epic)
- `neon reflections on wet surfaces` — 霓虹反光 (cyberpunk, urban night)

### Atmosphere
- `light rain with visible moisture in the air`
- `heavy fog reducing visibility to 20 meters`
- `dust particles visible in shafts of light`
- `cigarette smoke curling through the frame`
- `steam rising from grates and manhole covers`
- `autumn leaves scattering in wind gusts`

### Color Palette
- `desaturated cool tones`
- `warm amber and burnt orange palette`
- `high contrast black and white`
- `neon-soaked cyberpunk palette with teal and magenta`
- `muted earth tones with occasional color pop`
- `bleach bypass look with crushed blacks`
