# 风格变体

## 1. Neon Noir（霓虹黑色电影）

- 灯光：霓虹粉/紫/青为主，高饱和度反射湿地表面
- 相机：手持 + 慢快门拖影
- Master Style 替换：`Neon-lit noir handheld film sequence, shot on Arri Alexa 65, high saturation neon reflections on wet surfaces, natural film grain, photorealistic cinematic, T1.4 shallow depth of field`

## 2. Golden Hour Duel（黄金时刻决斗）

- 灯光：低角度暖金阳光，长投影，逆光轮廓
- 相机：手持 + 逆光眩光（lens flare）
- Master Style 替换：`Golden hour handheld film sequence, shot on Arri Alexa 65, warm low-angle sunlight with long shadows and backlight rim lighting, natural lens flares, photorealistic cinematic, T2.8`

## 3. Underworld Red（地下世界红光）

- 灯光：主导红/暗红实用光源，深黑阴影，极低照度
- 相机：手持 + 极暗噪点 + 呼吸感
- Master Style 替换：`Underworld red-lit handheld film sequence, shot on Arri Alexa 65, dominant red/dark-red practical lighting with deep black shadows, extreme low-key illumination, heavy grain, photorealistic cinematic, T1.4`

## 4. Cold War Surveillance（冷战监控）

- 灯光：冷白荧光灯，绿色调，无阴影扁平光
- 相机：模拟 CCTV 监控 + 偶尔 zoom in
- Master Style 替换：`Cold surveillance handheld sequence, shot on Arri Alexa 65, cold white fluorescent lighting with green tint, flat shadowless illumination, CCTV aesthetic, photorealistic cinematic`
