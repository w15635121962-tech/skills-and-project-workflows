# Jiuweihu Magic VFX Library

Use this reference for 《九尾狐驯养指南》 magic effects: particles, attacks, mark surfacing/dissolving, iris activation, holy spring light, dark curse mist, and four-clan energy colors.

Always combine this file with `user-standards.md`. The source material includes strong VFX words such as `8k`, `ultra-detailed`, large glow, and macro eye light. In this user's workflow, adapt those ideas into photorealistic, controlled, local effects: no full-screen light pollution, no self-lit eyes unless the shot explicitly calls for magical activation, no cheap game VFX, no random runes, and no large glow that changes the established scene lighting.

## Core Energy Colors

- Sera: green-gold alchemical magic. Use jade-green plus subdued gold, with medicinal/alchemical particle logic.
- Theron: moon-silver fox power. Use silver-white, pearlescent motes, cold moonlit veils, and weightless spiritual motion.
- Lucian: golden wind blade. Use gold wind, feather-like light shards, sharp air currents, and slashing arcs.
- Empress: sacred phoenix gold fire. Use imperial-gold phoenix ember-feathers and non-burning divine flame.
- Dark curse: black mist with grey-violet or sickly purple-red sunken glow. Keep it low, dim, and oppressive.

Color must remain character-consistent. Do not mix Sera green-gold, Theron moon-silver, Lucian gold, phoenix-gold, and dark curse violet unless the shot explicitly depicts collision or conflict.

## Three-State Link Rule

For major casting or pressure shots, the character's mark, iris state, and energy release should align in the same color family:

```text
印记低亮/燃亮 + 瞳膜同色魔力微亮 + 能量粒子同色凝聚/释放
```

Use the rule only when the shot truly needs magic activation. For quiet emotional shots, keep the effect smaller: mark low glow, very few particles, and natural eye reflection.

## Particle Texture Bank

Use these visible nouns and directions rather than abstract prose:

- subtle atmosphere or buildup: drifting glowing motes, floating light dust, luminous spores;
- release or attack: bursting sparks, scattering light shards, exploding embers;
- buildup or absorption: particles converging inward, light motes swirling to a core;
- holy/ritual light: volumetric god rays with motes floating in the beam;
- barrier edge or ending: particles continuously shedding and dissolving into air;
- energy absorption: ambient light points drawn inward to center.

Direction defines meaning:

- buildup: converging inward;
- release: bursting outward;
- absorption: drawn inward;
- sustained barrier: circulating along a membrane;
- dissolution: retreating inward and dissolving;
- corruption: clinging, sinking, and darkening.

## Layering Pattern

For a readable magic shot, use three visual layers:

1. outer layer: drifting or shedding motes;
2. core layer: a small glowing core, membrane, blade edge, or mark line;
3. motion layer: a short glowing trail, ripple, crackle, or dissolving edge.

Keep all layers anchored to a body part, prop, weapon, barrier, water surface, or mark. Avoid free-floating magic with no physical source.

## Light Magic Softening, Light Version

Use this whenever the user says the magic looks hard, plastic, sticker-like, dead-bright, opaque, or not dreamy enough. The goal is light softening, not blur: keep the effect clear and readable while removing the solid cut-out feeling.

Core rule:

```text
通透 + 软边 + 明暗核心 + 环境光溢 = 去硬；糊、暗、淡 = 过柔，不要。
```

Use these replacements:

- not opaque or solid: `translucent luminous energy, light passing through it`;
- not sticker-like: `soft edges, gentle bloom, subtle feathered rim`;
- not dead-flat bright: `a bright luminous core softly falling off toward feathered edges, brightest at center fading outward`;
- not floating over the image: `casting a soft colored glow on nearby skin, ground, rain, mist, weapon, or desk surface`.

General light-softening suffix:

```text
translucent luminous energy with light passing through it, a bright glowing core softly falling off toward feathered edges, gentle bloom, the magic light casting a soft colored glow on nearby skin and surfaces, clean and clear but not solid or sticker-like, subtle rim softness, crisp yet ethereal
```

Do not add these positive words because they make the effect too soft or unclear:

```text
dreamy blur, hazy, soft focus, faint, barely visible
```

Add or adapt these negatives whenever hard VFX is a risk:

```text
solid opaque energy, plastic light, sticker-like flat overlay, hard cut-out edges, pasted-on effect, dead-flat uniform glow, harsh neon, oversaturated blown-out glow
```

## Enemy Magic Attacks

Use these for hostile magic, beasts, minions, and monsters. Enemy energy should read as dark curse, corruption, undead, or void: cold black, grey-violet, purple-red, sickly green, or void-black with violet edge. Keep the contrast clear: enemy energy is dim, oppressive, light-absorbing, or sickly; hero energy is cleaner green-gold, moon-silver, gold wind, or phoenix-gold.

Enemy attacks should usually use a three-phase structure:

```text
[build] enemy energy gathers at maw, weapon, staff, core, arrowhead, or mist source;
[release] attack travels along a clear path toward the target;
[impact] the energy hits, is blocked, sinks in, collapses inward, or corrupts the ground.
```

Always state the attack path, target, and interception point when a hero blocks the attack. Avoid vague full-screen darkness.

### Dark Land Chaos Beast

Use for the script's dark land beast: a colossal chaos creature, wolf-like but not a normal wolf, wrapped in black mist, purple-red evil eye glow, able to keep moving with a ruined half-body silhouette.

#### Black Mist Devour

```text
[build] black mist and grey-violet motes gather inside the chaos beast's maw, purple-red throat glow kindling low;
[release] a forward torrent of black mist and shadow tendrils surges toward the target, sickly-green sparks scattering only along the edges;
[impact] light-absorbing shadow engulfs the target area or hits a shield surface, with oppressive darkness and low purple-red edge glow.
```

Controls: keep the mist directional, not a full-frame fog wall. The maw, travel path, target point, and impact surface must be readable.

#### Lunging Bite And Shadow Tear

```text
[build] the chaos beast coils its muscles, black mist trailing from its damaged body, violet eyes locking onto the target;
[release] it lunges forward with a savage bite, shadow tendrils whipping backward from the movement;
[impact] a localized purple-red shadow shockwave tears across the impact line, with dark motes bursting from the bite path.
```

Controls: no gore focus, no graphic exposed organs. Use silhouette, mist, shockwave, and stone impact instead of explicit body damage.

### Shadow Golem

```text
[impact] the void collapses inward on the target point, absorbing nearby light into a silent dark dent.
```

Controls: effect should pull light inward rather than glow outward. Keep violet edge glow thin and readable.

## Hero Versus Enemy Color Collisions

When hero and enemy energy collide in the same shot, keep both colors separated and readable:

- Sera green-gold alchemical magic versus chaos beast black-violet or purple-red mist.
- Theron moon-silver fox power versus necrotic green undead fire.
- Lucian golden wind blade versus dark sorcerer violet curse, with possible violet corruption creeping along gold as foreshadowing.
- Empress phoenix-gold fire versus undead, void, or dark shadow.

Collision pattern:

```text
hero energy travels from hero side, enemy energy travels from enemy side, the two colors meet at a clear collision line, sparks or motes scatter along that boundary only, neither color floods the whole frame.
```

Do not mix colors into muddy brown or generic white. If one side wins, state which color pushes the other back and where the defeated energy dissolves.
