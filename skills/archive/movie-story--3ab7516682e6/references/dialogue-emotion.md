# Cinematic Dialogue & Emotional Shot Design Protocol

电影级对白与情绪镜头设计协议

## Core Principles

- Dialogue scenes = emotional flow + power shifts + spatial oppression + psychological fluctuations in silence
- At the highest level, even if muted, the audience should read the emotion
- The camera must shoot "what was not said"

## Sightline as Emotional Attack (视线关系)

- Eye contact is emotional attack. Record who dares to look, who evades, who looks away first
- Advanced: shoot the pause/micro-movement BEFORE the verbal response. Delayed reaction creates tension

## Pauses & Silence (停顿与沉默)

- The most important part of advanced dialogue is often when no one is speaking
- During pauses: use steady shots or extremely subtle push-ins, keep ambient sound, let the audience "suffer" in the emotion. Cutting will destroy the emotional flow

## Spatial Distance = Psychological Distance (空间距离 = 心理距离)

- **Emotionally stable**: Medium shot, symmetrical, stable
- **Emotionally oppressive**: Characters close together + telephoto compressing space
- **Relationship collapsing**: Pull back + massive negative space, characters swallowed by the environment

## Subtext Shots (潜台词镜头)

Shoot "leak points of lies": hand leaving cup, breath pausing, eyes losing focus, light turning cold — NOT just talking faces.

## Emotional Power Shift (情绪主导权转移)

- **Powerful character**: Fuller frame, closer, more stable, low angle
- **Vulnerable character**: Obstructed, more negative space, at frame edge
- When power reverses, camera language MUST reverse synchronously (composition center, obstruction relationships, stability)

## Breathing Lens (呼吸镜头)

The lens has human psychological rhythm:
- Emotion approaching: slow push-in
- Retreating: stop
- Losing control: begin instability
- Advanced breathing lens is extremely subtle, like air flowing, driven by emotion not technique

## Cinematic Shot-Reverse-Shot (电影级正反打)

Use asymmetrical shot-reverse-shot to reflect emotional imbalance:
- Different focal lengths, different composition density, different negative space
- Powerful side: fuller and closer
- Vulnerable side: more negative space and obstruction

## Focal Length & Emotional Psychology (焦距与情绪心理学)

- **Wide (24-35mm)**: Alienation, unease, spatial oppression
- **Mid (50mm)**: Natural, balanced
- **Telephoto (85-135mm)**: Spatial compression, breathing difficulty, emotional closing-in, desire/confrontation

## Third-Person Perspective (第三视角)

"Space is watching them." Camera at room corner, outside door, end of corridor, in mirror. Creates sense of fate, observation, destiny. Ideal for scenes where relationships are about to collapse.

## Dialogue Scene Rhythm Structure

1. **Calm phase**: Medium shot + static camera + complete environment
2. **Probing phase**: Micro push-in + increased obstruction + breathing sense enhanced
3. **Oppressive phase**: Telephoto + close-up + spatial compression
4. **Explosion phase**: Handheld + fast cuts + broken composition
5. **Collapse phase**: Static + pull back + massive negative space + empty frame

## Director Style References (optional, selective)

- **Wong Kar-wai style**: Heavy spatial obstruction (door frames, mirrors, walls), the stronger the emotion the more restrained the camera, shoot brushing past, clothing edges, slow-motion turns
- **Nolan style**: Information oppression dominant, mid-close + hard rhythm, shoot logic warfare not melodrama
- **Kurosawa style**: Space itself participates in dialogue, characters shift power through walking, turning, occupying frame
- **Godfather style**: Silence accumulates, quietest before explosion, ambient sound (train) intensifies with emotion
