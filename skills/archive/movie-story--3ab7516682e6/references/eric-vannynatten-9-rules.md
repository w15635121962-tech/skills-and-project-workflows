## 九条法则 AI 映射

### 1. 要少不要多

> 元素越少越惊艳，主体越突出，画面越干净。

**AI 提示词映射**：

```
minimalist composition, single subject,
clean uncluttered background, negative space,
hero subject isolated, no distracting elements
```

**避坑**：提示词里不要堆砌过多元素，AI 会全部塞进去导致画面杂乱。

| 好坏对比 | 描述 |
|----------|------|
| ❌ 坏 | 「一个人、一棵树、一座房子、一条狗、一只鸟在天空中，远处有山...」 |
| ✅ 好 | 「一个人独自站在空旷的广场上，几何阴影将画面分割」 |

---

### 2. 色彩对比

> 用色彩对比让照片更有张力。颜色不要太多，同样遵循「少」原则。

**AI 提示词映射**：

```
limited color palette, 2-3 dominant colors,
strong color contrast, complementary color scheme,
no more than 3 colors in frame
```

**经典配色组合（AI 已验证可行）**：

| 组合 | 场景 | AI 提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 红+黑 | 戏剧张力 | `crimson red against deep black, dramatic contrast` |
| 蓝+橙 | 冷暖对比 | `teal and orange color grading, cinematic contrast` |
| 黄+紫 | 高贵氛围 | `mustard yellow against lilac purple` |
| 白+藏蓝 | 极简高级 | `crisp white against navy blue, clean minimalist` |

---

### 3. 动静对比

> 静态照片中体现动态，让画面不死板。

**AI 提示词映射**：

```
motion blur on moving element, sharp static background,
dynamic tension between movement and stillness,
freeze frame with motion trails
```

**四种动静对比模式**：

| 模式 | 描述 | 提示词 |
|------|------|--------|
| **人流穿梭** | 人物静止，人群动 | `one person still, crowd blurred in motion around them` |
| **车辆拖影** | 建筑静，车流动 | `stationary architecture, light trails of passing cars` |
| **水流丝绸** | 石头静，水流丝滑 | `sharp rocks, silky flowing water, long exposure` |
| **舞动定格** | 衣摆动，表情静 | `flowing dress in motion, calm composed face, dynamic stillness` |

---

### 4. 大小对比

> 大与小对比，增加画面冲击力。

**AI 提示词映射**：

```
scale contrast, tiny figure against massive architecture,
giant landscape dwarfing human subject,
monumental scale composition
```

**三组经典大小对比场景**：

| 场景 | 提示词 |
|------|--------|
| **人类 vs 建筑** | `tiny person standing before colossal brutalist building` |
| **人类 vs 自然** | `small lone figure surrounded by towering redwood trees, awe scale` |
| **人类 vs 城市** | `solitary silhouette dwarfed by giant skyscrapers, urban canyon` |

---

### 5. 明暗对比

> 有了明暗，层次感和主次才能出来。

**AI 提示词映射**：

```
high contrast chiaroscuro, dramatic light and shadow,
strong key light with deep blacks, 
split lighting, Rembrandt lighting
```

**三种明暗布局**：

| 布局 | 描述 | 提示词 |
|------|------|--------|
| **大面积暗+局部明** | 主体被光照亮 | `mostly dark frame, single shaft of light illuminating subject` |
| **逆光剪影** | 背光全暗轮廓 | `backlit silhouette, dark figure against bright background` |
| **侧光切面** | 半明半暗 | `side lighting, half face illuminated half in shadow` |

---

### 6. 引导线

> 利用公路、铁路、河流、栅栏作为引导线，体现纵深感和立体感。

**AI 提示词映射**：

```
strong leading lines drawing eye to subject,
converging lines perspective, 
road vanishing point composition,
railroad tracks receding into distance
```

**四类引导线 + 提示词**：

| 引导线类型 | 提示词 |
|-----------|--------|
| 道路 | `endless road converging to vanishing point, leading eye to lone figure` |
| 铁路 | `parallel railroad tracks disappearing into horizon, strong perspective lines` |
| 河流 | `winding river leading through valley, natural S-curve composition` |
| 栅栏/桥 | `row of fence posts receding into mist, bridge leading to distant horizon` |

---

### 7. 框架构图

> 前景中使用事物构建场景，"框中框"效果。可用：拱门、窗框、悬垂的树枝。

**AI 提示词映射**：

```
frame within a frame composition,
natural framing through arch window door branches,
depth created by foreground framing elements,
foreground frame silhouette
```

**四类框架元素**：

| 框架 | 提示词 |
|------|--------|
| 拱门 | `view through stone archway framing distant landscape` |
| 窗框 | `scene seen through open window frame, framing composition` |
| 树枝 | `overhanging tree branches framing the scene below, natural vignette` |
| 门框 | `figure silhouetted in doorway, door frame as natural frame border` |

---

### 8. 低角度拍摄

> "蹲着仰着别站着。" 向上垂直仰拍，与平常视角拉开差距。

**AI 提示词映射**：

```
low angle shot, worm's eye view,
looking up perspective, towering above,
ground level camera placement, dramatic upward angle
```

**四种低角度场景**：

| 场景 | 提示词 |
|------|--------|
| 建筑 | `low angle looking up at towering skyscraper, dramatic upward perspective` |
| 树木 | `worm's eye view, tree trunks shooting up into sky, canopy overhead` |
| 人物 | `low angle hero shot, powerful figure towering above camera` |
| 仰视天空 | `ground level shot looking straight up, trees/canyons framing sky` |

---

### 9. 窗口光

> 面向窗口，让窗口光为面部补光，最实用的光线。

**AI 提示词映射**：

```
window light portrait, natural soft light from window,
north-facing window light, gentle window illumination,
Rembrandt window lighting on face
```

**三种窗口光效果**：

| 效果 | 提示词 |
|------|--------|
| 柔光人像 | `soft window light falling on face, gentle natural portrait lighting` |
| 逆光剪影 | `subject backlit by bright window, silhouette against window glow` |
| 百叶窗条纹 | `venetian blind shadows across face, striped window light pattern` |

## 九律组合模板

### 综合模板（一次用3-4条法则）

```
# 人像街头
low angle shot (+法则8) 
of a lone figure (+法则1) 
framed by stone archway (+法则7) 
window light illuminating face (+法则9) 
deep shadows and bright highlights (+法则5)

# 城市景观
vanishing point road composition (+法则6)
tiny human dwarfed by buildings (+法则4)
motion blurred passing cars (+法则3)
teal and orange color palette (+法则2)
```

### 法则优先级（AI 生图建议）

```
构图优先 → 法则1「要少不要多」（先确定画面层级）
  ↓
透视 → 法则6「引导线」+ 法则7「框架构图」+ 法则8「低角度」
  ↓
光影 → 法则5「明暗对比」+ 法则9「窗口光」
  ↓
对比 → 法则2「色彩」+ 法则3「动静」+ 法则4「大小」
  ↓
精修 → 质感参数（film grain / oil painting / sharp focus）
```

## 快速检查清单

使用前核对：

- [ ] 画面元素是否 ≤ 3个主要对象？（法则1）
- [ ] 色彩是否控制在 2-3 种主色？（法则2）
- [ ] 是否有动静对比元素？（法则3）
- [ ] 是否有大小/尺度对比？（法则4）
- [ ] 是否有明确的明暗分区？（法则5）
- [ ] 是否有引导线指向主体？（法则6）
- [ ] 是否有前景框架框住主体？（法则7）
- [ ] 角度是否脱离平视？（法则8）
- [ ] 光源方向是否明确？（法则9）
