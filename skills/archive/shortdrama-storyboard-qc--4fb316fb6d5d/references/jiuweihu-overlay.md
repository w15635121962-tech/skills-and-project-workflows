## Character emotion corrections

### Sera

- Distinguish three kinds of cold: scarred cold toward Lucian, superior cold toward Ruby, outward-cold/inward-soft restraint toward Theron.
- Use shallow smile pauses, withdrawing/offering hands, a brief shoulder freeze, scales or contract-sigil changes, and distance changes.
- Never turn her into a frequently crying, hysterical, passive victim.
- Add coverage for `understands → suppresses → lays a plan → chooses` when missing.

### Theron

- Sera's presence and safety trigger the switch between soft/clinging and cold/protective.
- Let tails carry emotion: affection, possession, vigilance, and pressure need different tail states.
- In heart-blood pain, combine physiological agony with gentle willing devotion.
- Animate only 1–2 primary tails deliberately; keep the others in slight drift. Never make all nine tails move broadly at once.

### Lucian

- Early behavior is relaxed, magnetic, entitled, and careless; loss of control grows later.
- Show arrogance through casually damaging or discarding something precious.
- After terminal rage and blood tears, reduce performance into emptiness, hoarseness, and delay rather than endless shouting.

### Ruby

- Preserve the rapid switch between public wounded innocence and private triumph; the single tail reveals truth.
- Nine-tail pressure triggers instinctive fear. Never stage her as a high-rank queen.
- At judgment, retain genuine grievance from a weak survivor whose scheme failed; do not flatten her into a pure villain mask.

### Caspian, Empress, and elders

- Caspian releases tension through sharp humor and movement; remove comedy and create a sound vacuum around his decisive line.
- The Empress uses minimal movement for maximum authority: seated posture, pause, gaze, and room silence before exaggerated magic.
- Keep each elder's care distinct; do not express all warmth with the same gentle smile.

## Project-specific generation gates

- Keep the style medieval beastman fantasy, imperial palace, dark fairytale, and elegant beastman aristocracy with real stone, metal, cloth, skin, and restrained local magic. Reject xianxia, game-CG, or cheap animal-ear cosplay drift.
- Treat the blood contract, holy spring, heart blood, cedar barrier, dark-land magic, and recall mirror as locked recurring visual systems.
- Keep Ruby's single tail material, warm, and non-glowing; do not elevate it to Theron's nine-tail energy status.
- Resolve nine-tail geometry in the image layer first, then animate only limited tail motion in video.
- Route transformations through build → light-obscured topology change → settle.
